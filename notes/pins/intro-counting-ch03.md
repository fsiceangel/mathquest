# Pins — intro-counting ch03 (Correcting for Overcounting)

Read `notes/variation-spec.md` first. Every KEY below was brute-forced in node by enumerating the
objects (distinct permutations of a multiset, all pairs of a set, and — for every symmetry
item — all labeled arrangements canonicalized under the stated group). Do not retarget a pin;
if your own count disagrees, STOP and report. Answer indices are derived from the base:
v1 = (b+1)%4, v2 = (b+3)%4, v3 = (b+2)%4. Worksheet items are free response (no indices);
their keys are LaTeX strings, e.g. `'$210$'`.

Conventions to state in every stem: letters/digits/people/beads are distinguishable unless the
stem says "identical"; "row" means order matters and the row is never flipped; a number may not
begin with $0$ unless the stem calls it a code. **For every symmetry item the stem must say
explicitly whether rotations count as the same and whether flips count as the same** — e.g.
"rotations of a seating are the same seating; the table is never turned over", "bracelets that
differ by a rotation or by a flip are the same", "the pendant may be rotated but not flipped
over", "the chairs are all different, so rotating everyone gives a different seating".
