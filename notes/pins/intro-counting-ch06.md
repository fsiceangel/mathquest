# Pins — intro-counting ch06 (Some Harder Counting Problems)

Read `notes/variation-spec.md` first. Every KEY below was brute-forced in node by enumerating
the objects directly (permutations of labeled people, multiset arrangements, digit strings,
grid paths as point lists, subsets, tuples). Do not retarget a pin; if your own count
disagrees, STOP and report. Answer indices are derived from the base: v1 = (b+1)%4,
v2 = (b+3)%4, v3 = (b+2)%4. Worksheet items are free response (no indices); their keys are
LaTeX strings, e.g. `'$84$'`.

Every variation combines at least two techniques, as the base problems do (glue + complement,
fix-a-seat + restricted fill, casework + stars and bars, inclusion–exclusion + a fixed
position, bijection + casework, …). State every convention in the stem: people, books, beads,
and letters are all different unless the stem says "identical"; "row" means order matters;
"round table" means seatings that differ by a rotation are the same (say so); a "bracelet",
"necklace", or "key ring" also identifies flips (say so); "next to" means adjacent seats;
"directly opposite" needs an even number of seats; digit strings are positive integers with no
leading zero unless the stem says "code" (leading zero allowed); "at least one" boxes vs.
"empty allowed" is always spelled out; grid paths move one unit right or one unit up per step.
