# Pins — intro-counting ch11 (Expected Value)

Read `notes/variation-spec.md` first. Every KEY below was computed exactly in node over the full
outcome distribution (all 216 three-die rolls, all $\binom{n}{k}$ hands, every path of every
stopping tree) with BigInt rationals — no floating point, no closed-form shortcut taken on
faith. Do not retarget a pin; if your own value disagrees, STOP and report. Answer indices
are derived from the base: v1 = (b+1)%4, v2 = (b+3)%4, v3 = (b+2)%4. Worksheet items are
free response (no indices); their keys are LaTeX strings, e.g. `'$\frac{13}{5}$'`.

**Conventions every stem must settle explicitly:** whether an entry cost is subtracted (say
"expected net gain" when it is, "expected payout" when it is not); whether dice/coins/spinners
are fair; with or without replacement; when a multi-stage game stops; and whether a losing bet
forfeits the stake. Money stems take a two-decimal dollar key ($\$0.50$, $-\$1.40$); point,
count, and probability stems take an exact fraction. A negative expected value is written with
the sign outside, $-\$0.30$.
